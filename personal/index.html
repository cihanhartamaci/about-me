Philosophy

Temet nosce

Know thyself

Temet nosce is Latin for “know thyself” — ancient Delphic wisdom once carved at Apollo’s temple in Delphi.

It is a reminder to look inward before chasing the road ahead: stay honest about who you are, what you need, and what you leave behind.

For me it is less a slogan than a quiet compass — on Ordu shores, Istanbul nights, Sumela stone, and every stretch in between.
