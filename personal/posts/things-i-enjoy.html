Things I Enjoy

I'm a fan of experiences that make ordinary days feel a little less ordinary.

A good drive, discovering a new place, a late-night conversation, exploring something unfamiliar, or simply taking some time to clear my mind — sometimes, the simplest things are the most enjoyable.

I also appreciate good design, technology, cars, and anything that reflects creativity and attention to detail.

I don't think every interest needs to become a passion or every moment needs to be productive. Sometimes, enjoying something just because it makes you happy is more than enough.
