A Soft Spot for Cars

I've always found cars interesting for more than just getting from one place to another.

Design, proportions, driving dynamics, technology, and the character of a vehicle all make a difference. Some cars stand out because of their performance, others because of their presence on the road.

For me, the appeal is in the details — the way a car looks, feels, and makes an ordinary drive a little more enjoyable.

I enjoy comparing different models, discovering new designs, and thinking about what makes a car truly worth owning.

Because sometimes, the journey deserves just as much attention as the destination.
