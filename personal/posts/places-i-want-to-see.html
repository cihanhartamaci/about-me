Places I Want to See

For me, traveling is about more than visiting famous landmarks. It's about experiencing a different atmosphere, discovering local places, trying new food, and seeing everyday life from another perspective.

I'd love to explore cities with distinctive architecture, beautiful coastlines, scenic roads, and places where you can get lost for a while without worrying about the time.

Some trips are about adventure. Others are about slowing down and enjoying the surroundings.

My travel list is always evolving, and I hope to keep adding new destinations, unexpected discoveries, and stories worth telling.
